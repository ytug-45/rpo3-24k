Gordon Ramsay

Gordon Ramsay, Born November 8, 1966, is a British chef, restaurateur, television personality, and writer. His restaurants have been awarded a total of 16 Michelin stars, and in 2021, held nine of any chef in the world. He has hosted a number of culinary television shows, including *Hell's Kitchen* and *MasterChef*.

Andre Ruth

Andre Ruth, Born September 11, 1980, is an American celebrity chef and military veteran. He worked as an executive sous chef at the White House for five years, serving Presidents Obama and Trump. He is known for his unique culinary style that blends classic techniques with modern American flavors.
